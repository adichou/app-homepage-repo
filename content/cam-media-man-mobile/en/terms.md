# Terms of Use and Subscriptions

CamMan ZhuShou uses [Apple's Standard End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).

## Auto-renewable subscriptions

The monthly, six-month, and yearly Pro plans provide the same service and are billed as Apple auto-renewable subscriptions.

- Payment is charged to the Apple Account.
- The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- Users can manage or cancel the subscription in App Store account settings.
