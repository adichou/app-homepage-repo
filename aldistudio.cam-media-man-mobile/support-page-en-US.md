# CamMan Companion Support

CamMan Companion helps photographers safely ingest camera cards and Photos media on iPhone and iPad, then organize a tag-based shooting-reference library.

## Before an ingest

- A full camera-card workflow requires a source card and writable destination storage, usually connected through a compatible USB-C hub.
- Keep both devices connected until the ingest and any source cleanup are complete.
- For a first run, enable Test Mode to validate the workflow with 10 files.

## Source protection

- The app copies media and checks the destination copy.
- Failed, incomplete, or conflicting items remain at the source.
- Before cleaning an external source, the app rechecks each destination file. If the recheck fails, the corresponding source file is retained.
- Removing originals from Photos requires an iOS confirmation, and removed items remain recoverable in Recently Deleted.

## Common questions

### Why is Start Export unavailable?

Choose a valid camera-card or Photos source and a writable destination folder. The source card and destination cannot be on the same storage volume.

### What should I do after an interrupted ingest?

Open the app and review the report. Completed items remain recorded; unfinished or failed source items stay in place. Follow the report to retry, resolve a name conflict, or inspect remaining files.

### Why did web import find no images?

Enter a valid HTTPS webpage. Some sites block automated access, require sign-in, or render images only with dynamic scripts, so the app may not be able to extract candidates.
